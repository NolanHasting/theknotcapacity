About The Knot crowd tracker

Check how busy The Knot Climbing Gym in Gainesville is before heading over. The live count comes from the gym's public occupancy reading. Staff recounts periodically correct the count, while future crowds are estimates based on previous observations. Predictions can change as new data arrives.

This is an independent project, not the gym's official website. For hours, rates, events, and gym announcements, visit The Knot's official website.
